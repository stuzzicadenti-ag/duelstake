Platform Verified Accounts

Link your Steam, Discord, or gaming platform account. One account per player. Multi-accounting results in a permanent ban and forfeiture of funds.

⚖

Swiss Regulated

DuelStake is a skill-based competition platform, not a gambling service. Fully compliant with Swiss Federal Gaming Act (BGS) and EU regulations. Funds held in escrow by a licensed Swiss financial intermediary.

How payouts work: Stakes are held in escrow during the match. After both players submit proof, the AI verifies the result. If both proofs agree, the winner receives the payout instantly (minus platform fee). If there's a dispute, a human reviewer decides within 24 hours. All transactions are logged and auditable.
